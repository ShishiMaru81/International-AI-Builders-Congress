## Interview protocol (do not fabricate results)

1. Recruit ≥8 tomato growers and ≥2 extension officers in Gazipur or similar districts with informed consent.
2. Tasks: create/select plot, upload leaf photo, interpret uncertainty, set area, compare two markets, mark an action done.
3. Capture: time-on-task, completion, comprehension of DEMO vs LIVE, trust/distrust comments, Bangla wording issues.
4. Record anonymized notes only; no private leaf photos reused for training without separate consent + review.

## Pilot protocol

- N plots, fixed evaluation week, baseline paper/notebook workflow vs AgroSphere assisted workflow.
- Primary KPI: successful task completion rate and time-to-first attributable next step.
- Secondary: screening coverage on locked photo set; agronomist agreement on a labeled subset.
- **Do not** convert pilot arithmetic into crop-loss % without a pre-registered outcome definition.

## Sustainability notes

Free-host demos are ephemeral (sleep, photo loss, 30-day DB). Durable object storage, identity, retention and abuse limits are prerequisites before real private farmer data at scale.
